# EverVoice: pitch and demo checklist

## One-line pitch
EverVoice captures a grandparent's stories in their own language and gives them to the next generation, anywhere in the world, in English, Urdu and Arabic.

## 3-minute demo script
1. Problem (20s): elders are lonely, stories and dialects fade, 70% of the UAE is expat so many families live far from their elders.
2. Record (40s): tap the big button, tell a short story in Urdu or Arabic, show "The AI is listening".
3. Result (40s): transcript, translation switching between English, Urdu, Arabic, tags, mood, a follow-up question from the agent.
4. Storybook (30s): create the illustrated book, play the real voice, download the PDF.
5. Explore (30s): timeline, map, Ask Grandma (answers only from stories), Heritage Vault.
6. Care and privacy (20s): reminders, weekly summary, consent, delete-all, opt-in archive.

## Be honest on stage
- Say which voices are real and which are simulated. Demo Mode is labeled "simulated".
- Say transcripts can contain mistakes and families can review them.
- Say mood signals are gentle observations, not medical diagnoses.
- Say Ask Grandma is a memory search, never a fake grandparent.

## Ethics slide
Consent before recording. Private by default. Audio is never shared publicly. Opt-in text-only archive with withdraw. Delete everything button. No voice cloning. Human review of AI text.

## Judging criteria
- Impact: loneliness plus heritage loss, global diaspora.
- Agent behavior: it asks, remembers earlier topics, follows up, processes and organizes.
- Technical: voice to transcript to translation to storybook, RTL languages, free-tier stack.
- Scalability: more languages, care homes, museums, schools.
- Polish: warm design, large elder screen, demo mode.

## Before the stage (day before)
- [ ] Record 2-3 real stories with consent (or use clearly labeled volunteers).
- [ ] Process them in advance so transcripts, books and vault items already exist.
- [ ] Check ai.dev/rate-limit: do not spend your daily quota on testing.
- [ ] Open Demo Mode once as a backup and test voices on the stage laptop.
- [ ] Test microphone permission, Wi-Fi and the Vercel link on the stage laptop.
- [ ] Screenshot every key screen and record a backup video.
- [ ] Keep the shared demo account clean: delete test recordings.

## Roadmap slide
More languages (Hindi, Bengali, Tagalog), real voice-call agent, family invites, scheduled weekly emails, moderation for the archive, partnerships with care homes and heritage institutions.
